RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made on 01-April-2026 between Mr. Sharma (Landlord) and Ms. Meera (Tenant).

1. Property: 2BHK flat at Pune, Maharashtra.
2. Rent: Rs. 20,000 per month, payable by 5th of every month.
3. Security Deposit: Rs. 40,000. The deposit will be refunded within 15 days after vacating the property, after deducting any damage costs with receipts.
4. Notice Period: Either party can end this agreement by giving 1 month written notice.
5. Lock-in Period: 6 months. If tenant leaves before lock-in, one month rent will be charged.
6. Rent Increase: Rent may increase by maximum 5% after completion of 12 months.
7. Maintenance: Landlord is responsible for major repairs. Tenant is responsible for minor maintenance under Rs. 1,000.
8. Eviction/Termination: Landlord can ask tenant to vacate only with 1 month notice for valid reasons stated in writing.
9. Police Verification: Tenant will complete police verification within 7 days of moving in.
10. Utilities: Electricity and water bills will be paid by the tenant as per actual meter reading.
11. Inspection: Landlord may inspect the property with at least 24 hours prior notice.
12. Subletting: Tenant shall not sublet the property without written permission.
